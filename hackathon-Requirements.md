Here’s a concise summary of the hackathon requirements and final deliverables for ClarityGate.

## Hackathon requirements

- Build with Qoder as the central tool in the workflow.
- Show a real, working project, not just a concept.
- Keep the scope focused and hackathon-sized.
- Use a spec-driven workflow where possible.
- Make the project demo-friendly and polished.
- Center the story on how Qoder helps you ship faster or better.

## ClarityGate-specific product direction

- ClarityGate is a requirements-quality linter for spec-driven development.
- It catches vague, ambiguous, incomplete, or untestable requirements before Qoder Quest Mode builds from them.
- It should feel like a **professional spec-quality cockpit**, not a childish game.
- The core experience should include:
  - Spec Inbox.
  - Analyzer.
  - EARS Coach.
  - Quest Readiness Score.
  - Missions.
  - Impact View.

## Final deliverables

### Required submission items
- A social post on LinkedIn or X.
- A 2–3 minute demo video.
- A submission form entry containing:
  - the social post link.
  - the demo video link.

### Social post requirements
- Must mention the project.
- Must describe what it does.
- Must share your experience using Qoder.
- Must tag `@QoderOfficial` and `@AlibabaCloud`.
- Must include `#QoderHackathon` and `#BuildWithQoder`.
- Screenshots or a short clip are recommended.

### Demo video requirements
- Must be 2–3 minutes long.
- Must show the project in action.
- Must explain the problem it solves.
- Must demonstrate key features.
- Should be clear, short, and compelling.

## Judging criteria

- Use of Qoder: 30%.
- Innovation and creativity: 25%.
- Impact / post reach / engagement: 20%.
- Technical execution: 15%.
- Presentation and UGC: 10%.
